## Recording annotations

- Press an event's key, or click its on-screen button, while the video plays or is paused.
- **Point** events record one time. **State** events start on the first press and end on the second. An active state is highlighted in the event list.
- With subjects active, one annotation is written per active subject. Subjects cannot be changed while a state annotation is open; end the state first.
- Everything is saved immediately. `Escape` returns to the file list, and the video reopens where you left off.

## Correcting annotations

Right-click an annotation in the State or Point Annotations list:

- **View Details**: see and edit the event, times, and notes. Edited annotations are marked as manually edited.
- **Skip to Annotation**: jump the video to its start.
- **Reopen State**: clear a state's end time, then press its key at the right moment to end it again.
- **Delete**, or select several rows and choose **Delete Selected**. Undo with `{MOD}+Z`.

Click a column header to sort a list, and drag a header edge to resize a column.

## Buttons below the annotation lists

- **Coding Segment**: set the start and duration (or end) of the part of the video to score. Playback pauses at the end of the segment and offers to end any open states.
- **Visualize**: a timeline of all annotations, which can be exported as an image.
- **Event Keys** and **Subjects**: edit or switch the event and subject keys.
- **Mark Video Complete**: mark the video as finished (green check in the file list).

## Settings menu (gear)

Show or hide the floating playback controls, the audio track, the spectrogram, the events and subjects buttons, the zoom button, the Subject column, and the video name.

- **Audio and Video Settings**: brightness, contrast and other picture settings, volume, audio delay, pitch, skip intervals, and the audio track height.
- **Appearance**: theme, colors, and transparency.
